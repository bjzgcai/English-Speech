Stage report · 阶段报告

从首次尝试
到稳定练习 From first attempt
to sustained practice

Game 已形成稳定的重复练习行为。四周内，94 名学生完成 834 次作答， 累计实际口述约 25 小时。下一阶段的重点，是提升低活跃学生的覆盖面， 并降低学习行为高度集中在截止前的波动。

The weekly Game has developed a stable practice habit. Across four rounds, 94 learners submitted 834 answers and spoke for roughly 25 hours. The next opportunity is to widen participation and smooth the strong deadline concentration.

01 / Executive readout

核心结论 What the data says

Participation is broad enough to be habitual, completion is strong once learners start, and repeated attempts are producing measurable score gains.

01

参与已经具有黏性
Participation has become habitual

90/94 名学生至少参与两周，69/94 连续参与全部四周。第四轮仍有 78 名活跃学生， 相比首轮保留约 88.6%。

90 of 94 learners joined at least two rounds; 69 joined all four. The fourth round retained 78 active learners, or about 88.6% of the first-round total.

02

开始之后，几乎都会完成
Starting leads to submitting

95 名学生生成过题目，其中 94 名实际提交，按人去重后的进入→提交转化率为 98.9%。题目生成次数与保存次数之间的差异，主要来自重复生成和重录。

94 of 95 learners who entered submitted an answer. The gap between prompt generations and saved answers is driven by repeat starts, not a leak of learners.

03

复杂任务暴露表达短板
Harder tasks reveal delivery gaps

个人最佳分均值为 79.7。后期加入三分钟研究陈述和论文摘要任务后，周度最佳分 回落，Coherence 与 Grammar 下降最明显；这不应被直接解读为英语能力退步。

The mean best score was 79.7. Later rounds introduced longer and more complex tasks, so lower Coherence and Grammar scores should not be read as a direct decline in ability without a difficulty-controlled comparison.

05 / Learning signal

练习有效，但分数需要谨慎解读 Practice works; scores need context

Retakes produce strong within-week gains. Cross-week changes are confounded by task difficulty.

Practice effect / 练习效应 86.7–95.2%

每周作答至少两次的学生中，此比例在首次作答后提高了当周最佳分； 提升中位数为 7–14 分。

Of learners with two or more attempts in a round, 86.7%–95.2% improved from first attempt to weekly best, with a median gain of 7–14 points.

Weekly personal best / 周度个人最佳分均值

从 78.0 回落至 73.3，并不等同于能力退步。连续四周参与的 69 人中， Coherence 下降约 8.3 分，Grammar 下降约 5.2 分；同期任务从常规两分钟题 转向研究陈述、论文标题与摘要等复杂表达。

The decline from 78.0 to 73.3 coincides with a move toward longer research statements and paper abstracts. Among 69 four-round learners, Coherence fell by about 8.3 points and Grammar by 5.2 points.

07 / Next actions

下一阶段建议 Recommended next actions

Move from proving adoption to tightening participation and feedback quality.

  1. 01

    把中位数与个人最佳分作为核心 KPI
    Use median attempts and personal best as headline KPIs

    保留总次数和均值，但避免极端活跃账号掩盖大多数学生的真实体验。

  2. 02

    针对 Coherence 与 Grammar 做定向反馈
    Coach Coherence and Grammar explicitly

    在复杂任务后增加结构提示、句式复盘和可直接执行的修改清单。

  3. 03

    在截止前 48 小时提醒并分散负载
    Send a T-48h reminder and spread peak load

    结合周末与晚间行为，提前推送主题准备和提交提醒，降低最后一天集中提交。

  4. 04

    补充正式名单后计算全班参与率
    Add official-roster participation rates

    当前人数以实际参与学生为分母；拿到名单后才能识别未参与及低活跃学生。

Method & data notes / 口径说明

可复现，也有边界
Reproducible, with clear limits

  • 统计窗口：2026-09-28 00:00 至 2026-11-02 12:00，北京时间，四个完整轮次。
  • 学生按 unionId 优先、openId 回退去重；不展示学生姓名。
  • 只有带 challengeId 的 Game 作答被计入；11 月 2 日中午开启的新轮次不计入。
  • 静态快照，数据截至 2026-11-02 15:12；后续提交不会自动改变本页数字。
  • 当前缺少正式班级总人数，因此不能计算全班参与覆盖率。
