Practise speaking with AI.

Three practical ways to speak more often, receive useful corrections, and build a more natural English response.

Three ways to practise

A useful speaking coach should give you time to finish your thought, then help you improve what you said.

01

Doubao

Use English Practice mode.

Doubao's English Practice mode is designed for pronunciation and grammar correction. It lets you finish your response before giving feedback, so you can speak without being interrupted.

  1. Open the voice interface and tap Select Scenario.
  2. Choose English Practice.
  3. Finish your response, then review the corrections.
02

Yuanbao

Select Oral practice.

Yuanbao also includes a dedicated Oral practice mode. Use it for spoken conversation and corrections during the session.

  1. Start a voice conversation.
  2. Open the conversation-mode menu.
  3. Select Oral practice and begin speaking.
03

ChatGPT

Combine Voice with Custom Instructions.

ChatGPT offers Voice conversations, but it does not currently present a dedicated mode labelled Oral practice like the options above. You can create a similar coaching routine with Custom Instructions.

Set it up

  1. Open Settings.
  2. On web or desktop, choose Personalization. On mobile, choose Customize ChatGPT.
  3. Turn on customization and paste the coaching prompt.
  4. Start a Voice conversation and answer in English.
ChatGPT Voice guide Custom Instructions guide

Coaching prompt

Copy into Custom Instructions
You are my English speaking coach. Let me finish my full answer before giving feedback. Wait until I say, “I'm finished.” Then point out the three most important pronunciation, grammar, or word-choice problems. Give me a corrected, natural version of my answer, explain each correction briefly, and ask one follow-up question. Do not interrupt me while I am speaking. Keep the conversation in English unless I ask for Chinese.
